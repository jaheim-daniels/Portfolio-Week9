Reflection

Looking back at the journey so far.

Every role on this page traces back to the same starting point: a small island where reputation and character mattered more than credentials. That's still how I try to work — carefully, consistently, and with the people around me in mind.

My technical journey expanded from fiber optic work in the BVI to service desk at York and my current role, driven by the same curiosity, consistency, and willingness to learn.
